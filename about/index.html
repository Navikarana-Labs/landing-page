01 / The lab

Navikarana Labs is an independent research and engineering lab founded and operated by Lokesh Kumar N.

I started Navikarana as a home for investigating difficult technical problems and building ambitious technical work. The lab intentionally does not restrict itself to one field; the problem should determine the tools and disciplines it needs.

The long-term idea is simple: strong technical work can become open-source technology, a product, or an independent venture. Navikarana provides a common home for that work, with room for individual projects to develop identities of their own.

This is a founder-led effort, still taking shape. Projects will be documented on the work page as they develop, with short progress notes in updates.

My detailed engineering writing and personal work remain at loke.sh. For questions about the lab, write to hello@navikarana.io.
